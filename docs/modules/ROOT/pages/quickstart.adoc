= Quickstart
:description: From an empty ~/.scoutjobs to the first application in one session.

This walks through one full loop with Claude Code. In Codex the commands are `/scoutjobs-setup`, `/scoutjobs-search` and so on; in Cursor or Gemini say the sentence in the right-hand column.

== 1. Tell it who you are

Drop your current CV into `~/.scoutjobs/cv/` (PDF, DOCX, Markdown or text), then:

----
/scoutjobs:setup
----

The `scoutjobs-setup` skill reads the CV, writes `profile.md` (facts about you: roles, dates, skills, numbers) and asks what you want: remote or not, locations, salary floor, roles to avoid. Those become stated preferences with confidence 1. Everything a skill later writes into a resume or cover letter must exist in `profile.md`, so take a minute to correct it.

Start the dashboard in another terminal and keep it open:

[source,console]
----
$ scoutjobs serve
dashboard at http://127.0.0.1:7070
----

== 2. Check the CV once

----
/scoutjobs:cv
----

Two scores out of 100 (ATS readiness, content quality), a verdict, up to eight gaps. Fix the structural ones in the master CV now (two-column layout, missing section titles, no metrics); the per-job ones are handled later by `resume-tailor`. The result lands on the Profile & CV page.

== 3. Find jobs

----
/scoutjobs:search senior go engineer, remote EU
----

Public ATS APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Personio), RSS feeds and remote boards, no keys, deduplicated by URL and title, salaries converted to `currency`, added as `new`. Put the queries and your target companies in `config.yaml` (`search_queries`, `search_boards: greenhouse:stripe, lever:netlify`) so a bare `/scoutjobs:search` repeats them. Paste a URL or the posting text for anything else.

== 4. Score and decide

----
/scoutjobs:fit
----

Every `new` job gets a 0-100 score with three reasons and moves to `ranked`. The skill asks which to apply to and which to skip. A skip is logged with your reason in your words; that is what the learner reads on Friday.

== 5. Tailor and write

----
/scoutjobs:apply 3
----

Writes `resume.md` and `cover-letter.md` into `~/.scoutjobs/applications/<company>_<title>/`, both reviewed by `draft-reviewer` against `profile.md`, and marks the job `drafted`. Rewrite one piece with `/scoutjobs:tailor 3` or `/scoutjobs:cover 3`, and check the tailored resume against the posting with `/scoutjobs:cv --job 3`. The job page shows the four kit steps turning green. See xref:cv-and-cover-letter.adoc[].

== 6. Send

Set `email_from` and `smtp_host` in `config.yaml`, export `SCOUTJOBS_SMTP_PASSWORD`, then:

----
/scoutjobs:send 3
----

The skill builds the e-mail (cover letter as body, tailored resume attached), shows all of it, and asks in a full sentence. Only your yes in that conversation sends. The job moves to `applied` and the follow-up timer starts. See xref:apply-by-email.adoc[].

== 7. Every morning

----
/scoutjobs:brief
----

Interviews in the next 48 hours, follow-ups due, drafts waiting, new jobs, one learning task. Under 15 lines. On Friday `/scoutjobs:review` closes the week and runs `learn-preferences`.
