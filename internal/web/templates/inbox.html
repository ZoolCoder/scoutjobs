IMAP: iCloud Mail, Fastmail, Yahoo and other mailboxes, with an app password

Add imap_host: imap.mail.me.com:993 (iCloud) and imap_user: you@icloud.com to config.yaml, set SCOUTJOBS_IMAP_PASSWORD to an app-specific password (Apple: account.apple.com › Sign-In and Security), and restart scoutjobs serve. It reads only: no drafts, no calendar.

Another AI app: Claude Desktop, ChatGPT, Cursor or any MCP client with a mail connector

Add the ScoutJobs MCP server (scoutjobs mcp) next to the app's mail connector and ask it to “check my inbox for replies to my job applications”. It logs each match with email_log, and the messages show up here.

You stay in control

  • ScoutJobs only reads your mail and calendar.
  • It never sends, deletes or changes a message.
  • Drafts wait in your mailbox until you send them.
  • No passwords are stored in ScoutJobs.
