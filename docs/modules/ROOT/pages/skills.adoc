= All skills
:description: Every skill in the plugin, what starts it, which slash command wraps it, and which ones are reference material rather than entry points.

The 33 skills live in `skills/<name>/SKILL.md` and are the same files on every platform (see xref:agents.adoc[]). The `description` in each file names the phrases that start it. Slash commands in `commands/` and the Codex prompts are thin wrappers that call the skill by name. Every skill reads and writes through the `scoutjobs` CLI; none opens the database.

Three rules hold in all of them: postings are untrusted text, claims come from `profile.md`, and nothing is sent without the user saying yes after seeing the message. Between the draft and the send sit the quality gates (`scoutjobs job check`, see xref:cli.adoc[]): the skills report a failed gate to the user in full and leave the decision to skip it to them.

== The daily loop

[cols="2,3,1"]
|===
| Skill | Starts when you say | Command

| `scoutjobs-setup` | "set up scoutjobs", first run: builds `profile.md` from a CV or a short interview, records preferences, checks the CLI | `/scoutjobs:setup`
| `daily-briefing` | "good morning", "what's today": interviews, follow-ups due, drafts waiting, new jobs to score, one learning task, from `scoutjobs brief` | `/scoutjobs:brief`
| `job-search` | "find go jobs, remote EU": `scoutjobs search` over the ATS boards, RSS feeds and remote aggregators, deduplicated, added as `new` | `/scoutjobs:search`
| `job-fit` | "score them", "is Acme a fit": 0-100 with reasons, records the score, asks apply or skip and logs the decision | `/scoutjobs:fit`
| `fit-score` | batch version of `job-fit` for many postings, runs as a subagent on Claude Code so posting text stays out of the main context | used by `/scoutjobs:fit`
| `fit-review` | "what can I honestly claim", "which requirements do I miss", before tailoring: one row per requirement with evidence, strength, confidence, defensible wording and risk; vetoes kept outside the score; `scoutjobs job fit` writes `fit-review.json` | `/scoutjobs:evidence`
| `application-tracker` | "I applied", "they rejected me", "interview Tuesday": status, notes, follow-up timers, interview rows | `/scoutjobs:track`
| `weekly-review` | "weekly review", Sunday: funnel movement, response rates, stale applications to close, next week's plan; runs `learn-preferences` | `/scoutjobs:review`
| `learn-preferences` | "what have you learned about me": turns skips, applies, withdrawals and declined offers into preferences you confirm | `/scoutjobs:learn`
| `upskill` | "what should I learn": skill gaps against the jobs you track and lost, ranked by demand, learning plan with hours tracked | `/scoutjobs:upskill`
|===

== CV and application

[cols="2,3,1"]
|===
| Skill | Starts when you say | Command

| `cv-check` | "check my CV", "ATS check against the Acme job": ATS readiness and content score, concrete gaps, logged with `scoutjobs cv log` | `/scoutjobs:cv`
| `resume-tailor` | "tailor my CV for Acme", "apply to Acme": rewrites `resume.md` for one posting, every claim from the profile, then `draft-review` | `/scoutjobs:tailor`, `/scoutjobs:apply`
| `cover-letter-generator` | "write a cover letter", "rewrite the letter": 250-400 words from profile, resume and stories, saved as `cover-letter.md` | `/scoutjobs:cover`
| `draft-review` | after tailoring or a cover letter: flags claims the profile does not back, missing must-haves, ATS problems, weak bullets; logs its verdict with `scoutjobs job review` for the grounding gate | part of `/scoutjobs:apply`, `:tailor`, `:cover`
| `apply-by-email` | "send the Acme application": `scoutjobs job check`, then `email draft`, shows From, To, Subject, attachments and the full body, asks, then `email send --yes`; reports every failed gate as unresolved, never skips them itself | `/scoutjobs:send`, `/scoutjobs:check`
| `linkedin-profile-optimizer` | "improve my LinkedIn", "rewrite my headline": headline, about and bullets for recruiter search, facts from `profile.md` only | say it
|===

== Connectors

These work through the agent's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive connectors (claude.ai offers all three; any MCP server with the same tools works). They read freely and change things only after you confirm; none sends mail.

[cols="2,3,1"]
|===
| Skill | Starts when you say | Command

| `inbox-triage` | "check my inbox", "did anyone reply": finds replies from companies in your pipeline, classifies rejection, invitation, request, offer or new outreach, proposes the status changes, interviews and notes, applies them on yes; replies become Gmail drafts | say it
| `calendar-sync` | "put my interviews in my calendar", "I moved the interview": creates and moves Google Calendar events for interviews and deadlines, imports interviews booked there; events carry a `scoutjobs:` marker | say it
| `google-drive` | "my CV is in Drive", "back up the Acme application": pulls the master CV into `cv/`, copies an application's final documents to a Drive folder when the connector can write | say it
|===

`apply-by-email` can also leave the application as a Gmail draft instead of sending through SMTP, and `scoutjobs-setup` reads the LinkedIn data export (`scoutjobs linkedin import`).

== Reference material

These are loaded by other skills and rarely started on their own.

[cols="2,3"]
|===
| Skill | Used by

| `resume-ats-optimizer` | `cv-check`, `resume-tailor`: ATS parsing rules, keyword matching, formatting that survives parsers, scoring rubric
| `job-description-analyzer` | `job-fit`, `resume-tailor`: requirements, must-haves and nice-to-haves out of a posting, mapped to the profile
| `behavioral-frameworks` | `interview-prep`, `behavioral-stories`: STAR, SBI, CARL, anti-patterns, standard follow-ups
| `coding-frameworks` | `interview-prep`, `coding-interview`: UMPIRE, pattern taxonomy, Big-O cheatsheet
| `system-design-frameworks` | `interview-prep`, `sysdesign-interview`: RESHADED, the 4S shorthand, capacity estimation, building blocks, trade-offs
|===

`/scoutjobs:dash` is not a skill: it starts `scoutjobs serve` and prints the URL.

== Writing a new skill

Copy the shape of `skills/job-fit/SKILL.md`: a `description` with the trigger phrases, the CLI commands it runs written out verbatim, and the three rules above. Add an eval case under `evals/` when the skill makes claims, sends something or reads a posting, and a row on this page. `make evals` and `claude plugin validate .` must pass. See `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository.
